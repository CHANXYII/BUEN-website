About Us

The School of Engineering at Bangkok University prepares world-builders for the engineering profession, balancing theory, practice and real-world projects in fully equipped labs. Our students learn both hardware and software, along with related technologies such as IoT, AI & Machine Learning, and keep up with the new data era through Big Data, covering Programmer, IT, Smart System, Robotics & Automation. Our distinctive curricula build skills that set you apart from typical engineering programs, skills you can really use. Through partnerships with international universities, including faculty/student exchanges and joint research projects, students work alongside international researchers and gain a global outlook. With 5 majors to choose your own path, we are an institution that produces engineers ready to meet the technology needs of today and tomorrow!

Our 5 majors

  • Computer and Robotics Engineering

    Programming, IoT, AI and robotics, with hands-on hardware and software.

  • Artificial Intelligence Engineering and Data Science

    Build and deploy AI systems, from data to real business solutions.

  • Unmanned Aircraft System and Technology Engineering

    Drone hardware, software, autonomous navigation and mapping, with a drone pilot certificate.

  • Electrical Engineering

    Power systems, machine control and automation, with AutoCAD, PLC and renewable energy skills.

  • Intelligent Systems and Digital Innovation Engineering

    Hardware, software and AI combined into smart systems, AIoT and digital twins, plus creative media.
